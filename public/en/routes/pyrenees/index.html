The route

The Pyrenees route is an experience designed to discover the region on two wheels.

We are not looking for kilometres just for the sake of riding. We are looking for roads worth riding, landscapes worth stopping for, and places worth discovering.

The route combines mountain passes, secondary roads, small villages and local gastronomy.

The goal is to enjoy the motorcycle, but also everything that happens off the road.
