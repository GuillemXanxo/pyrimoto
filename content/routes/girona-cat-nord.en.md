This is a route for those who enjoy leaving the usual roads behind, letting the landscape surprise them and feeling that something new can appear around every bend. The Empordà plain and the Pyrenees are the starting point for a journey that takes us across a border and, with it, into a change of landscape, light, architecture and even the way we experience the mountains. One of those experiences where, without covering huge distances, you somehow feel that you've travelled much farther.

Crossing the Pyrenees by motorcycle has something special about it. It's about leaving the familiar behind and entering a territory that feels close and, at the same time, surprisingly different. It's about seeing the same mountains from the other side, discovering a new perspective and letting the contrasts give the journey even more meaning. For those looking for roads that invite you to ride at an unhurried pace, landscapes that make you want to stop the bike, and that feeling of having experienced something that goes far beyond the miles you've ridden.

**The route is designed to be ridden over 3 full days, but the length of each day can be adapted.**
